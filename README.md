# MathyRoad

An endless lane-hopping game. Cross roads, rivers and train tracks, but every hop forward costs a turn, and you earn turns by solving math problems.

**Play:** https://hunterawsome1243.github.io/MathyRoad/

No install and no build step. You can also download the repo and open `index.html` in any browser.

## How to play

- Answer a math question to earn 5 turns (store up to 10, more with upgrades).
- Spend one turn per hop forward. Moving sideways is free.
- Time freezes while a question is open, but you can't move until you answer or close it.
- Stand still too long and the giant eraser gets you.
- Grab coins and spend them in the Upgrade Shop. Your coins, upgrades and high score are saved in your browser.

## Controls

| | Keyboard | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Move sideways | A / D or arrows | Swipe, or tap beside the pencil |
| Hop forward | W or up arrow | Swipe up, or tap above the pencil |
| Earn turns | E | Earn turns button |
| Answer | 1-4 | Tap an answer |

## Files

`index.html`, `style.css`, `game.js`. Everything is drawn on a canvas with simple shapes.
